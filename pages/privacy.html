Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 Version 1.0

The less we hold, the less there is to leak. This page describes exactly what we have, what we do not have, and what we could not give away even under pressure.

The short version

No account, no email, no password on our side, no identity documents. Your wallet key is encrypted in your browser and never reaches us. What we do have is ordinary server logs and whatever the blockchain already makes public.

1.What stays on your device

The following is kept in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your machine unless you copy it out yourself:

  • Your encrypted wallet — the private key, encrypted with your password using PBKDF2 and AES-GCM. We store the ciphertext only, and only on your device.
  • Your password — never stored anywhere, in any form. It exists only in memory while you are unlocking.
  • Interface preferences — such as your last bet amount.

Clearing your browser data erases this. If you have not backed up your private key, the wallet is unrecoverable.

2.What we can see

  • Server logs — our hosting provider records IP address, browser user agent, requested page and timestamp for every visit. This is standard for any website and is used for security and debugging.
  • On-chain activity — wallet addresses, transactions and bet outcomes recorded on Solana. This is public to everyone, not just to us.

3.What we never have

  • Your private key or seed phrase.
  • Your wallet password.
  • Your name, address, date of birth or identity documents.
  • Your email address or phone number.
  • Your payment card or bank details.

We cannot disclose what we do not hold. That is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight.

4.The blockchain is public and permanent

Everything you do on-chain — deposits, bets, withdrawals — is visible to anyone forever, and no one can delete it. Not us, not you. If you fund your wallet from an exchange account in your own name, that link can be followed. Treat your wallet address as public information.

5.Third parties

  • Hosting — the site is served by our hosting provider, which processes the server logs described above.
  • Solana RPC providers — reading balances and broadcasting transactions exposes your IP and wallet address to the RPC node.
  • Fonts and libraries — some assets are loaded from public CDNs, which see your IP address.

Use a VPN if this matters to you. We would rather tell you where the leaks are than claim there are none.

6.Cookies and tracking

We do not set advertising cookies and we do not sell data. Local storage is used for the wallet and your preferences, as described above.

7.Your choices

  • Delete everything we store on your side — clear this site's data in your browser settings. Back up your key first.
  • Use a fresh wallet — nothing links one wallet to another unless you link them yourself.
  • Stop at any time — there is no account to close.

8.Children

The platform is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect anything from minors. If you believe a minor is using the site, tell us.

9.If this policy changes

The date at the top changes and material changes are announced on X. We will not quietly start collecting more than what is listed here.

Privacy question?

Ask directly. If the answer is uncomfortable, we will still give it.
