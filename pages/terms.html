1.Who runs pepe.fail

What that means for you in practice: there is no regulator you can escalate a dispute to, and no licensing body supervising us. If that is not acceptable to you, do not use the site. We would rather lose a player than mislead one.

The only official support channel is @pepebetsupport on X. Anyone contacting you as pepe.fail from anywhere else is not us.

2.Who may use the site

  • You must be at least 18, or the legal gambling age where you live, whichever is higher.

  • You must be using your own wallet and playing for yourself, not on behalf of someone else.

We do not run identity checks today. That places the responsibility on you, and we expect you to take it seriously.

3.Your wallet and your keys

pepe.fail is non-custodial. When you create a wallet here, the private key is generated in your browser, encrypted with your password and stored on your device. It is never sent to us. We never see it, and we could not hand it over even if we were asked to.

There is no password reset and no account recovery. If you lose your password and your key backup, the funds in that wallet are gone permanently. Nobody can restore them — not us, not anyone.

Clearing your browser data deletes the encrypted wallet stored on your device. Back up your private key before that happens.

4.Bets and results

  • Every game carries a house edge, and it is published on each game page. Over time, the house wins. That is how a casino works.
  • If a bug, a display error or a broken game produces an obviously wrong result, we may void the affected rounds and return the stakes. We will say publicly when we do this and why.
  • If the site, your connection or the network fails mid-round, the outcome recorded on-chain is the one that counts.
