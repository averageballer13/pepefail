Responsible Gaming

Last updated: August 2026

The house edge is real and it never sleeps. Play long enough and the maths wins. That is not a warning label we are forced to print — it is how the product works, and you deserve to know it before you bet.

The short version

Gambling is entertainment you pay for, not a way to make money. Only stake what you can lose without it changing your week. If you are betting to win back what you lost, stop today.

1.Signs it has stopped being fun

Be honest with yourself about any of these:

  • You bet more than you planned, or more often than you planned.
  • You are chasing losses — betting bigger to get back to even.
  • You gamble with money meant for rent, food, bills or debt.
  • You borrow, sell things or use credit to keep playing.
  • You hide how much you play from people close to you.
  • You play to escape stress, boredom or low mood.
  • You feel restless or irritable when you try to stop.
  • Playing has cost you sleep, work or a relationship.

One of these is worth watching. Several of them together is a problem worth acting on now, not later.

2.Rules that actually work

  • Decide the amount before you open the site, and treat it as spent the moment you start.
  • Keep a separate wallet for playing. Never fund it from savings.
  • Set a clock. Long sessions are where discipline goes to die.
  • Never chase. A loss is the price of the entertainment, not a debt to recover.
  • Do not play impaired — tired, drunk, angry or upset.
  • Withdraw wins. Money left in play returns to the house eventually.

3.Taking a break

Because pepe.fail is non-custodial and has no accounts, we cannot lock you out of your own wallet — no operator can. What works instead:

  • Move your funds out to a wallet you do not keep on this device.
  • Block the site with a blocker that needs a password someone else holds. Gamban and BetBlocker cover gambling sites specifically.
  • Tell someone. Secrecy is what keeps the cycle running.
  • Register with a self-exclusion scheme in your country if one exists.

If you ask us to close your access, we will do it — but understand that a self-custody wallet means the real control is on your side, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.

4.Where to get help

These are independent organisations. They are free, confidential, and not connected to us in any way.

GamCare / BeGambleAware Free advice and treatment, UK-based. begambleaware.org
Gambling Therapy Free online support worldwide, many languages. gamblingtherapy.org
Gamblers Anonymous Peer support groups, online and in person. gamblersanonymous.org

If you are in immediate crisis, contact your local emergency services or a suicide prevention line. Money problems pass. Please do not make a permanent decision about a temporary loss.

5.Under 18

Nobody under 18 may use this platform. If you share a device, protect it with a password and consider parental control software that blocks gambling sites.

6.What we will not do

  • We will not tell you that gambling is a way to earn.
  • We will not push you to deposit more after a loss.
  • We will not hide the house edge — it is on every game page.
  • We will not market to anyone who has told us they have a problem.
