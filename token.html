What the token is for

It funds the bankroll

A casino cannot pay a big win it does not hold. Part of every fee goes into the bankroll, which is what sets the maximum bet and the biggest payout the house can actually cover.

It turns volume into buybacks

Creator fees accrue on every trade, up or down. That revenue does not depend on the price going up — it depends on the token being traded.

It pays players to hold

Holding $FAIL raises your rakeback and unlocks higher limits. The perk is a discount on your own play, not a cut of anyone else's.

The flywheel

  1. 1

    Trading generates fees

    Every buy and sell pays a fee. The creator share routes to the public treasury — on the bonding curve, then on the AMM after migration.

  2. 2

    Fees split in two

    Half is spent buying $FAIL back on the open market. The rest grows the bankroll and covers operations.

  3. 3

    A bigger bankroll means bigger games

    More reserves means higher max bets and larger top payouts. That is the product improving, not a marketing line.

  4. 4

    Players generate house edge

    Real wagering produces real revenue. A share of it is spent buying $FAIL back, on top of the buybacks already funded by trading fees.

  5. 5

    Buybacks bring attention

    Every buyback is published on-chain. Visible, recurring buy pressure is what pulls volume back to step one.

Step 5 feeds step 1 — that is the loop

How buybacks work

Bought, then locked

Buybacks are executed on the open market from the treasury wallet, on a fixed daily schedule rather than at a discretionary moment. Tokens bought are locked in the bankroll vault, where they back player payouts instead of being resold.

Verifiable, not announced

The treasury address is public. Every buyback is a transaction anyone can check, and the running total is published on this page. A buyback that cannot be verified on-chain did not happen.

Holder tiers

Tiers are read from your connected wallet balance. Rakeback is a rebate on your own wagering — it is not a share of platform revenue or of other players' losses.

Honest status

The flywheel only spins if people actually play. Trading fees alone will not carry it — without wagering volume, a buyback is just the treasury spending its own money. The casino has to be worth using first; the token compounds it, it does not replace it.
